# YouTube-to-RSS

A personal podcast feed made from audio from selected YouTube videos.

## Language

**YouTube video**:
The source video selected to become an audio episode.
_Avoid_: Episode

**Submission**:
A request to turn a selected YouTube video into an episode.
_Avoid_: Episode

**Episode**:
A playable audio item derived from a selected YouTube video and published in the feed.
_Avoid_: Video

**Feed**:
The collection of published episodes to which a podcast app subscribes.
_Avoid_: Queue

**Publication**:
The point at which a completed audio episode becomes available through the feed.

**Podcast player**:
The application the listener uses to subscribe to the feed and play its episodes. Pocket Casts is one example.
_Avoid_: Client

**Retention period**:
The period after publication during which an episode remains available from the feed.

**Expiry**:
The end of an episode's retention period, after which the service no longer offers it.
_Avoid_: Played, downloaded
